Coral reef management trade‑offs: an expert preference simulator

Build up to three reef management plans and see which one coral reef experts would choose. Results come from a discrete choice experiment with 136 scientists, managers, and restoration practitioners who each judged ten trade‑off scenarios.

Shares use a first‑choice rule: each expert is assigned to the available option with the highest total part‑worth utility from their individual hierarchical Bayes estimates (ties split evenly). Utilities are the raw CBC/HB estimates, so None is scored by each expert’s own none‑option utility. Confidence intervals are mean ± 1.96 × SE across the selected experts.

How to read the cost attribute. Cost is expressed as the share of the total budget left for traditional activity management (marine protected areas, water quality, fishing regulation) after funding the outplanting, biobanking and R&D in the plan.
